RUN EU BIP  ·  Limerick, Ireland

Welcome to TUS

Technological University of the Shannon Moylish Campus

For five days, our group of students from across the RUN EU alliance came together in Limerick, Ireland for a Blended Intensive Programme focused on engineering applications in Python. Hosted at the Technological University of the Shannon's Moylish Campus, the week combined hands-on workshops, collaborative projects, and time to explore the city.

TUS is Ireland's newest technological university, formed in 2021 from the merger of Limerick Institute of Technology and Athlone Institute of Technology. The Moylish Campus is the principal Midwest campus, home to over 7,000 students and located just outside Limerick city centre.

This blog documents the trip day by day — one student per day, each with their own perspective on what we got up to.

Day 1  ·  Monday

Arrival & First Impressions

Written by Jentimir Eduard Limerick City

Our first day of the BIP Python programme began with our arrival in Limerick, Ireland. After travelling from our home countries, we settled into our accommodation and had our first opportunity to explore the city.

Limerick immediately impressed us with its friendly atmosphere, historic buildings, and lively streets. Walking through the city centre gave us a first glimpse of Irish culture and helped us get familiar with our surroundings before the programme officially began.

In the evening, we met the other participants and programme organisers. This was a great opportunity to introduce ourselves, learn about different cultures, and start building connections with students from across Europe.

The welcoming environment helped everyone feel comfortable and excited for the days ahead. The day concluded with group activities that encouraged teamwork and communication.

Although it was only the first day, we already gained new experiences, met interesting people, and looked forward to the Python workshops and collaborative projects that would follow during the programme.

Written by Jentimir Eduard

Day 3  ·  Wednesday

Midweek Adventures

Written by Student 3 Limerick City

During our Python course in Limerick, it was great to take a break from coding and explore some of the city. One of the highlights was visiting King John's Castle, where we got a glimpse into Limerick's medieval past while enjoying beautiful views of the River Shannon.

Later, we stopped by The Locke Bar, a traditional Irish pub known for its welcoming atmosphere and strong local character. It was a nice change of pace from our studies, giving us the chance to unwind, spend time together, and experience a bit of Irish culture beyond the classroom.

Day 5  ·  Friday

Final Day, Presentations & Farewell

Diana Sitnic TUS Moylish Campus

The final day of the programme focused on presenting the projects we had been developing throughout the week. Each team demonstrated their Python application, explained their design choices, and shared the challenges they encountered during development.

Password Project
Cipher Project
Gambling Project
Skin Analysis Project

The presentations showcased a variety of topics, like data analysis, machine learning, and security applications. It was interesting to see how different teams to see Python applied in unique ways.

After the presentations, we reflected on everything we had learned during the programme. Beyond the technical skills, the week focused on providing an opportunity to collaborate with students from different countries, share experiences, and build international connections.

As the programme came to an end, we said our goodbyes. The week in Limerick turned out to be a valuable experience that combined learning, teamwork, and cultural exchange!
