RUN EU BIP  ·  20–24 April 2025

Engineering Applications
in Python

Written by Lukas Clarysse

From the 20th of April through the 24th, our group of students from across the RUN EU alliance came together in Limerick, Ireland for a Blended Intensive Programme titled Engineering Applications in Python.

Hosted at the Technological University of the Shannon's Moylish Campus by Professor Sajjad Sajjadi — who made for a terrific host, offering both ambiance and expertise, in a technical sense but also life in general — the week gave everyone a hands-on, practical environment without the pressure that such opportunities usually come with.

That freedom allowed us to spend our energy on the international aspect of it: meeting so many fascinating people from Portugal, Finland, Ireland and beyond. These interactions are priceless, and gave us a better insight into how different cultures engage with each other and what each can learn from the other.

This blog documents the trip day by day — one student per day, each with their own perspective on what we got up to.

Day 1  ·  Monday

Arrival & First Impressions

Written by Jentimir Eduard Limerick City

Our first day of the BIP Python programme began with our arrival in Limerick, Ireland. After travelling from our home countries, we settled into our accommodation and had our first opportunity to explore the city.

Limerick immediately impressed us with its friendly atmosphere, historic buildings, and lively streets. Walking through the city centre gave us a first glimpse of Irish culture and helped us get familiar with our surroundings before the programme officially began.

In the evening, we met the other participants and programme organisers. This was a great opportunity to introduce ourselves, learn about different cultures, and start building connections with students from across Europe.

The welcoming environment helped everyone feel comfortable and excited for the days ahead. The day concluded with group activities that encouraged teamwork and communication.

Although it was only the first day, we already gained new experiences, met interesting people, and looked forward to the Python workshops and collaborative projects that would follow during the programme.

Written by Jentimir Eduard

Day 2  ·  Tuesday

Project kickoff

Written by William Plancke Limerick City

The moment I'd been looking forward to for months had finally come around. I was off to Limerick, Ireland, on a student exchange programme — a trip that promised new friends, interesting academic challenges, and the chance to experience a culture I'd only ever read about. Stepping off the plane and breathing in that crisp Irish air for the first time, I felt a mix of excitement and nerves that I wouldn't have swapped for anything. Tuesday was our official arrival day at the university, and from the moment we set foot on campus, it was obvious this was going to be a pretty special experience. The grounds were gorgeous — a mix of modern buildings and wide green spaces that felt perfect for both getting work done and just switching off for a bit. The staff were genuinely welcoming, helping us get through all the registration bits, showing us around the facilities, and doing their best to make us feel at home even though we were miles from our own.

Once all the admin was sorted, we each got stuck into our individual projects. This was really the whole point of the exchange — the reason we'd applied, packed our bags, and made the trip out to this part of Ireland in the first place. Settling into my spot in the university library for the first time, surrounded by students from all over quietly getting on with their own work, I felt properly motivated. There's something about being in a completely new environment that makes you want to knuckle down, and I was already feeling the benefit of it. That said, the day wasn't all about hitting the books. As the afternoon wound down, a group of us headed out to have a proper look around Limerick city. What hit me straight away was just how much character the place had — it's lively and unpretentious, with a real sense of history around every corner. We walked along the River Shannon, taking in those wide grey waters, and wandered past King John's Castle, which looked incredible standing there against the modern buzz of the city around it.

We ducked into cosy cafés, had a browse through some local shops, and stopped to listen to a busker playing the fiddle on a busy street corner — one of those little moments that just feels so perfectly Irish. The locals were brilliant, chatty and happy to point us toward their favourite spots without a second thought. By the time we got back to campus that evening, we were tired but absolutely buzzing. One day in, and Limerick had already made quite the impression.

Written by William Plancke

Day 3  ·  Wednesday

Midweek Adventures

Written by Bram De Craene Limerick City

During our Python course in Limerick, it was great to take a break from coding and explore some of the city. One of the highlights was visiting King John's Castle, where we got a glimpse into Limerick's medieval past while enjoying beautiful views of the River Shannon.

Later, we stopped by The Locke Bar, a traditional Irish pub known for its welcoming atmosphere and strong local character. It was a nice change of pace from our studies, giving us the chance to unwind, spend time together, and experience a bit of Irish culture beyond the classroom.

Day 4  ·  Thursday

polishing & presentation prep

Written by Jonas Vandenberghe Limerick City

For the entirety of this week, I had the pleasure of spending my time alongside my close friends Bram and William. On Thursday morning, we caught the bus at around 08:30 and arrived at school just before 9 o'clock. Since the classroom wouldn't open until 10:30, we made our way to the computer lab and used the extra time productively, continuing to work on our projects.

Once 10:30 arrived, all the students involved in the project gathered together, and each group resumed work on their assignments. My group was focused on implementing several additional features into our console-based mini-games program, which we had been developing in Python throughout the week. It was a satisfying process, seeing the project slowly come together with each new addition.

Around 1 pm, nearly all the students decided to take a well-deserved break and headed to the cafeteria together, where we enjoyed a delicious meal of fries and chicken smothered in curry sauce. It was a fun and lively atmosphere, with everyone sharing stories and laughs before heading back to work.

By 2 pm, we returned to the classroom to wrap up our remaining code-related tasks and shifted our focus toward preparing our PowerPoint presentations for the following day. The afternoon passed quickly, and before we knew it, 5 pm had arrived. We packed up our things, left the school, and made our way to a pub in the city centre for a few well-earned drinks.

After a couple of rounds, we decided to take one final stroll through the streets of Limerick, soaking in the atmosphere and savouring our last moments in this wonderful city. Once we had thoroughly enjoyed our evening walk, we headed back to the hotel, where we unwound in the swimming pool and sauna — a perfect way to relax after a long and productive day.

To round off the evening, we went out for a late dinner before settling into the hotel garden with a tasteful Irish beverage in hand, reminiscing about the incredible week we had shared together. It was a fitting end to what had truly been an unforgettable experience.

Written by Jonas Vandenberghe
